## Name

Voidy

## Short description (tagline)

A lightweight system cleaner for rootless & roothide.

## Description

Voidy frees up storage and keeps your jailbroken device tidy – with a clean, native interface that feels right at home on iOS.

**Cleaning**
- App caches, Safari cache, temporary files, crash reports and logs
- Downloaded .deb files, package manager caches and tweak caches
- Repository lists (optional)
- Privacy: browsing history, cookies & website data, keyboard learning – always with confirmation, never automatic
- Exclude individual apps, e.g. ones that keep offline music or maps in their cache

**Smart Insights**
- Health score after every scan
- Spots processes that keep crashing and points you to your tweaks
- Flags apps with huge caches and warns when storage runs low
- Everything is evaluated on your device – nothing is sent anywhere

**Tweaks**
- See every tweak with its package and target apps
- Toggle tweaks on and off without deleting anything
- "Disable all" for troubleshooting, then restore with one tap

**Tools**
- Large file finder with swipe to delete
- Remove orphaned packages
- Remove unused languages from jailbreak apps and tweaks
- Start and stop jailbreak launch daemons (core services are protected)
- Respring, rebuild icon cache, userspace reboot, ldrestart and reboot

**More**
- Automatic cleaning in the background, from every 6 hours to weekly
- Cleaning history with chart
- Home screen quick actions: Smart Clean and Respring
- Native dark interface, English & German

**Safety**
Voidy only removes files from clearly defined locations. Photos, messages, contacts, keychain, mail and the package database are never touched. When you uninstall Voidy, every tweak and daemon it disabled is re-enabled automatically.

**Compatibility**
iOS 15 and later · rootless and roothide
